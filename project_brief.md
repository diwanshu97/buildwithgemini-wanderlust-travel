# My agent: Wanderlust Travel Concierge
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps travelers discover destinations and craft personalized day-by-day itineraries with a catalog of curated sights, activities, and dining spots.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Traveler preferences (favorite travel pace, dietary restrictions, preferred trip types, budget preferences, and cities visited)
- Tools: Sights & restaurant lookup, weather forecast checking, day-by-day itinerary saving
- Catalog/UI: Curated attraction & restaurant cards with ratings, tags, and price tiers rendered via A2UI
- Image gen: Photorealistic postcard previews and scenic landmark visuals for recommended destinations
- Sandbox: Daily budget calculations, expense splitting, and currency conversion

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Google Maps Places integration, code sandbox for currency & budget math, Cloud Trace for latency tracking
